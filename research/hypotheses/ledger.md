# Test ledger — multiple-comparisons accounting

Each hypothesis family accumulates a count of experiments run against it. A
finding must cite its number ("test #N in family F"), and significance is read
in light of that count — the more tests a family has burned, the stricter the
bar for the next "discovery" (rough correction: p-threshold ~ 0.05/N, or an
honest caveat in the report). Slices examined WITHIN one experiment (features ×
horizons × cost levels) are declared in the report itself — gate #5.
