# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, be constructive, assume good faith, and keep
disagreements technical. Harassment, personal attacks, and bad-faith conduct
are not tolerated.

Additionally, given the subject matter of this project:

- **No pump-and-dump promotion, shilling, or financial solicitation** in any
  project space. Discussion of assets stays technical and methodological.
- **No presenting fabricated or cherry-picked results.** Misrepresenting
  experimental evidence is treated as a conduct violation, not just a review
  comment.

Report violations privately via the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Maintainers may warn, moderate, or ban at their discretion.
